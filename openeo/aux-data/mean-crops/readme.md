Mean cropland probability from Google Dynamic World, 
exported via Earth Engine and stored as float32.

Source collection: [GOOGLE/DYNAMICWORLD/V1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_DYNAMICWORLD_V1) (`crops` band).
Temporal mean over 2024–2025 at 10 m resolution.
CRS is EPSG:4326.
